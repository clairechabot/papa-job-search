# Voice sample — how Marcel writes (Vern: match this)

The excerpts below are from his real cover letter to AML Oceanographic
(VP Finance, July 2026). This is the voice standard for everything Vern
drafts: plain, confident, concrete; names his own perceived gaps before
the reader can and flips them; commits to specifics (the First-100-Days
pattern); zero buzzwords.

## The gap-flip (signature move)

> "I will flag directly what you will notice on my resume: my recent track
> record is enterprise-scale, and this role needs someone who personally
> builds the forecast. My career actually began in small, hands-on seats -
> Industrial Controller at a single Michelin plant, Divisional Controller
> at JD Irving, and co-owner of a small business. Getting my hands into
> the details is my zone of comfort."

> "I don't have Metronomics, EOS, or Scaling Up on my resume by name, but
> I've run their mechanics: at Michelin's Shared Services Center, I set
> 90-day priorities with weekly standup meetings, measurable targets,
> cascaded them into team-level KPIs, and used visual boards to track
> progress in real time. I will pick up AML's specific management tempo
> and vocabulary quickly."

## The First-100-Days pattern (reuse the structure, adapt the content)

> "Days 1-30 - Listen and Learn: I will spend the first month with the
> Controller, the AP/AR team, and key members of the team to understand
> the business, the customers, the goals, and the challenges... I will
> also review expectations directly with the Leadership Team [and the PE
> sponsor when there is one]."
>
> "Days 30-60 - Finance function Progress Plan: ...a diagnostic of the
> finance function's strengths and the areas that need improvement, broken
> into quarterly, measurable targets and validated by the Leadership
> Team... prioritize the reliability of the weekly cash, EBITDA, and
> full-year landing forecast, making sure the monthly financial pack lands
> consistently ahead of the leadership meeting with facts and analysis
> that answers 'what should we do,' not just 'what happened.'"
>
> "Days 60-100 - Build Forward: deliver the first full operating-plan
> cycle end to end, propose a right-sized FP&A roadmap... and put a
> succession and development plan in place for the Controller and finance
> team."

## Style rules extracted

- First person, active verbs, short declaratives; almost no adjectives.
- Specific numbers and named systems over claims ("weekly cash, EBITDA,
  and full-year landing forecast" - not "robust reporting").
- The motivation paragraph connects THIS company's mission to what he
  wants; never generic.
- Closes with fit + staying power, not flattery.
- ASCII hyphens only; no em-dashes; no exclamation marks.
