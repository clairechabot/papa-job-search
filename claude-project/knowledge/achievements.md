## Template (copy per story)

### [Short title, e.g. "ERP migration without missing a close"]
- **Situation**: [context, scale, stakes]
- **Task**: [what he owned]
- **Action**: [what he actually did — decisions, not attendance]
- **Result**: [number, timeline, consequence]
- **Good for**: [interview themes this answers — change management,
  cost control, team leadership, crisis, systems]

## Prompts to jog memory

- A cost reduction or margin improvement he drove (how many $?)
- A downturn/crisis he steered finance through (2008? COVID?)
- A system implementation or automation
- A team he built, restructured, or saved
- A negotiation (banks, suppliers, auditors, HQ)
- A capital project he financed or evaluated
- A time he said no and was right
- Something he fixed that nobody had managed to fix
