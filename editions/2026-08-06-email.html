A note from Vern

Consider the fractional route not as a lesser berth but as a side door held open. The construction post on Boston's South Shore deserves an application as well. Keep both lines in the water. Might I recommend you write to two Halifax advisory or private equity contacts this week, offering interim finance capacity in plain terms. Make it so.

Yours, Vern
